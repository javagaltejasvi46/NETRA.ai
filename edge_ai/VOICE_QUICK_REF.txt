└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  ./START_HERE.sh

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ HOW IT WORKS                                                                 │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  1. System starts → Plays welcome message
  2. Listens for 5 seconds → You can ask initial question
  3. Telemetry arrives → System analyzes and speaks decision
  4. Listens for 5 seconds → You can ask about the situation
  5. Repeat for each telemetry update

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ EXAMPLE QUESTIONS                                                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  "How far is the enemy?"
  "What is the threat level?"
  "Where is the hostage?"
  "What was the last decision?"
  "What is my heart rate?"
  "Is it safe to move?"
